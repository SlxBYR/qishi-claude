【sway】

This is ONE image containing exactly 16 closely spaced in-between drawings in an EXACT UNIFORM 4-column by 4-row grid, reading left to right then next row. Square 2048x2048 canvas if possible. Every cell is exactly one quarter of image width/height. No visible grid, no borders, no labels, no text. TRUE TRANSPARENT RGBA background. Full body comfortably inside EACH cell, equal scale in all cells, same camera, head top at same local y, hair and shoes never touch adjacent cells. Every character occupies about 84% of cell height. Maintain pixel-registered center/head position across all 16 drawings. Draw one coherent seamless SWAY ANIMATION LOOP of her being held in the air by an invisible grip at crown: legs bent with both shoes dangling, both hands clutching the same book at chest. All 16 frames are DISTINCT incremental drawings, NOT 4 repeated poses. FRAME HEAD/BODY TILT degrees about fixed crown anchor in reading order: 0,-2,-4,-6,-7,-6,-4,-2,0,+2,+4,+6,+7,+6,+4,+2. Feet gently swing on the opposite side of the crown; long hair tips and skirt hem trail by about two frames, adding believable soft follow-through. Tiny knees and hands adjust incrementally. Consistent surprised little O mouth and same amber eyes throughout, no expression flipping. Lock identical facial features, eye size, copper-orange long hair, orange flower and black ribbon/tassels on VIEWER RIGHT at all times, cream scholarly embroidered dress with asymmetric orange panel on viewer RIGHT and black panel viewer LEFT, lace, brooches, dark book and brown shoes. Never mirror her outfit. Match original chibi line art, flat soft cel shading and warm palette exactly. No props added, no cursor, hands, hooks, strings, shadows, ground, particles or motion lines. The point is genuine frame-by-frame fluid small movement: adjacent drawings should nearly overlap. The last frame must transition naturally to first frame. Prioritize pose consistency and equal cell registration over varied illustrations.

Claude 娘拖动动画原画与拆帧说明

原画
• grab-atlas.png：抓起，4×4，共 16 幅。
• sway-atlas.png：悬空轻摆，4×4，共 16 幅。
• drop-atlas.png：放下，4×4，共 16 幅。
三张原画均由图像生成工具绘制，保留原始透明 PNG；原图尺寸均为 1254×1254。
各组顺序均为从左到右、从上到下。

运行时素材
位置：../Qishi/Resources/Claude/drag-motion/
文件：dragging-grab-00.png 至 dragging-grab-15.png；sway、drop 同样各 16 帧，共 48 帧。
每帧统一为 364×364 RGBA 透明 PNG，脸部参考中心对齐在 (176,136)。
同目录 alignment.json 保存原图裁框、脸部参考坐标、归一化倍率与输出文件对应关系。

拆帧方式
原画透明外边距与格间距不规则，未按四等分直接切图。
使用 alpha 连通主体确定 16 个角色，并将低透明边缘按最近主体分配；分割时不阈值删除像素、不去边、不重绘。
sway 保持 1:1 原生像素，仅作整数平移；逐像素核对通过，全部 1,088,567 个非零 alpha 像素保留。
grab 与 drop 各使用全组固定 1.08 倍等比规范化，以接近 sway 的脸部尺寸；使用预乘 alpha 双线性采样，保留原生透明轮廓，未逐帧改变缩放倍率。
统一画布比最初建议的 344 多留 20 像素保护边距，避免裁去原画中极浅的透明散点；所有输出帧四边均为透明。
原图中已触及画布右边或底边的部分仅按原图保留，不补画、不猜测缺失像素。
